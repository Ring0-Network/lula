## 1. Exact games and claim

**ASSUMED — source game.** Work in HJ[h] with one fixed, public, complete 256-bit algorithm h, unmodified even inside coherent computations of a quantum adversary A; independent persistent F: valid ADRS22 -> Node16 and J: complete messages -> Node16; source-uniform independent public seed S; actual key-generation root; classical atomic signing responses; honest logical caches containing nodes/counters; permanent exposure across restores; and absorbing nonwinning source setup/signing noncompletion. Secret seed-bearing persistence fixtures and disclosure of private internal chain records are excluded. The game exposes logical API results, not the bytes of private internal hash calls. Input-length-based source operation counters can be charged even when an unavailable input's internal evaluation is represented by its challenge result. Arbitrary secret-dependent timing leakage is not established by this logical-game lemma.

**CITED — ordinary primitive interface.** HK22 Definition 3 uses uniform hidden X, reveals its Th(P,T,X) image in stage one and P only in stage two, and accepts any final preimage. Definition 4 SM-UD gives one real Th(P,T,X) or an independent uniform output, with X hidden and P released in stage two. Definition 7 additionally offers stage-one collection access at disjoint tweaks. The programs below use zero collection queries in stage one, hence satisfy that restriction. Here Th_128^h(S,T,x)=prefix128 h(S16||zero48||T22||x16). No private/full lower limb is needed; public evaluations still return actual full h outputs.

**PROVED — candidate result.** A stopped sequence of internal-node hybrids permits ordinary one-target SM-UD hops for every WOTS interior position. The hops change only private designated chain records/consumers in an analysis experiment. They never change h, including its actual predecessor cells or its coherent implementation. Together with one ordinary PRE target in the final hybrid, they bound the final classical H_final event. The distinguisher must decide a larger selected-position event, not attempt to decide which unknown earlier input was the canonical first H witness.

## 2. Selector and stopped virtual-chain experiments

**COMPUTED — namespace.** WOTS keys per layer are (2^41,2^36,2^31,2^26,2^21), chain counts (32,32,32,32,16), widths (16,16,16,16,256). The genuine nonterminal type-0 step namespace has

D_W=480(2^41+2^36+2^31+2^26)+4080*2^21=1,089,588,072,284,160.

The FORS secret/height-zero namespace is D_F=2^41*17*512=19,140,298,416,324,608. Their disjoint union has D_H=20,229,886,488,608,768, between 2^54 and 2^55. Draw 55 uniform bits. Decode valid labels into a WOTS (layer,tree,kp,chain,p), 0<=p<=w-2, or a FORS (tree,kp,j,i) with p=0. Dummy selectors make one fixed legal challenge query when needed for exact p=1 primitive syntax, then output failure/bit 0 without invoking source A. All address selection is independent of S. No full namespace is allocated.

**PROVED — stopped experiment G_k, k=0,...,254.** Fix a valid selector. For a selected WOTS chain let x_0 be its genuine private F value. Independently sample persistent Node16 coins x_1,...,x_min(k,p); these are the designated internal replacements. For i>=min(k,p), continue x_(i+1)=Th(S,T_i,x_i). All selected-chain honest consumers use this one trajectory; all other source consumers use their ordinary trajectories. Compute all WOTS endpoint compressions, subtrees, child values, canonical counters, root, message routing and logical cache payloads consistently from these records. A receives exactly this experiment's actual generated root. FORS selectors have no replacement sequence: x_0 is their private F secret and Y_0 its genuine leaf image. Public h remains unchanged at every input.

**PROVED — complete-response guard.** For a WOTS selector, assemble each requested response using opaque slots if necessary, complete every required counter row and source public computation, and if this COMPLETE response would serialize the selected chain at digit e<=p, stop without releasing any part of it. For a FORS selector, guard any complete response that serializes its selected secret. On noncompletion follow the source's absorbing nonwinning convention; do not condition on successful rows. Only released complete responses update Seen/exposure. No internal private-label lookup, tree computation or attempted early serialization triggers this guard by itself. All cache nodes/counters are computable from the known selected endpoint/image and do not expose internal x_i bytes.

**PROVED — final selected event E_k.** After a nonstopped complete run, execute the source final verifier with the ordinary public h and require actual root equality, freshness and acceptance (Win). E_k occurs if ANY reached classical type-0 call at selected T_p has payload x_p, or, for a FORS selector, the reached selected height-zero call has payload x_0. A's internal public/coherent computations are neither observed nor measured. Define E_k=0 on dummy, guarded, noncompleted or nonwinning branches. Never require the selected call to be the globally first H witness.

**PROVED — source-to-selected-event averaging.** G_0 is the original HJ[h] source experiment until its guard, since it replaces no chain node. On every source Win AND H_final run, choose its canonical first final hidden coordinate as a selector witness. At that WOTS position p there was no earlier completed honest digit e<=p; otherwise x_p would be in its addressed forward closure. At that FORS coordinate no complete response exposed the selected secret. Therefore choosing that label does not trigger the guard, source setup/root/cache values remain identical, and the genuine final call gives E_0. Extra selected coordinates may also contribute. Thus

Pr_HJ[h][Win AND H_final] <= 2^55 Pr[G_0:E_0],

where the right experiment includes the 55-bit selector. It is irrelevant whether A learned a numerical value by another route; exposure is indexed by coordinate, not global value membership. The selected guard is part of a reduction experiment, not an added source API rejection.

## 3. Legal hop k -> k+1

**PROVED — active hop, selected WOTS p>k.** B_UD,k chooses the selected step T_k in stage one and asks its sole ordinary UD target. After P=S is released, it runs every source A phase including the pre-key phase while withholding S/root from A until the source release point. In the real challenge branch its target Y is Th(S,T_k,X); in the random branch Y is uniform Node16. If k=0 put opaque X at the selected F label. If k>0 sample selected F and earlier replacement coins x_1,...,x_(k-1) normally, and put opaque X at the replacement coin x_k. Set x_(k+1)=Y and compute every later chain position by the actual h.

**PROVED — source/hybrid law.** In the real branch X is exactly the independent uniform private input x_k of G_k. Its next designated image is the actual h image Y, hence every designated endpoint/root/cache/counter and released response has G_k's joint law. In the random branch introduce a latent independent uniform X for the hidden private x_k if the challenger did not sample one; Y is independent uniform, exactly the newly replaced x_(k+1) of G_(k+1). All earlier coins and F/J labels have their unchanged joint distribution, and later values follow the true h. This is a coupling of analysis-game private variables, not a write into a public h table or a claim that an original h-derived x_k was uniform. At k>0, x_k is uniform because the preceding k replacements are already part of G_k.

**PROVED — unavailable-input audit.** B never needs to reveal X or evaluate its actual predecessor hash. For k>0 the internal step k-1 has a designated overwritten output X, so an honest chain endpoint/signature routine skips that private evaluation and uses the opaque slot; it does not answer any public request with X. Every complete selected-chain response at e<=p is guarded, including e=k and earlier positions. Every unguarded response has e>p>=k+1 and is computable from Y. Ordered node vectors, real pk.root, authentication paths and counters depend only on the computable endpoint. A's public h at T_(k-1), on any guessed or coherent input, computes its actual unchanged value, even when it differs from the internal replacement. The simulator intercepts only designated honest private chain consumers, never arbitrary verifier or adversarial h calls.

**PROVED — lawful distinguishing predicate.** Since p>k, x_p is known: it is Y when p=k+1 and otherwise a known suffix value computed from Y. B can decide Win and the existence of a final classical T_p call with payload equal to x_p, without X or a genuine-prefix equality test. It outputs this E bit. Therefore its real/random acceptance probability difference equals the active selected component of Pr[G_k:E_k]-Pr[G_(k+1):E_(k+1)]. There is no measurement of A's quantum h state. A predicate asserting the globally first H witness would require comparison with unknown earlier inputs and is not used.

**PROVED — inactive/dummy hops.** For p<=k, FORS, or dummy selectors, G_k and G_(k+1) are identical for that selector. B_UD,k makes a fixed legal one-target UD query and then outputs bit 0 without running A. This discards equal contributions from both branches. For active labels it uses the program above. Its overall real-minus-random bit gap is therefore exactly the full selector-averaged event-probability difference. Absolute value is bounded by the ordinary one-target SM-UD advantage, with zero stage-one collection access. All 254 hops are valid even though individual selectors cease changing earlier.

## 4. Final PRE embedding and telescoping

**PROVED — terminal distribution.** In G_254 every selected nonterminal WOTS x_p with p>0 is an independent replacement coin; p=0 remains the independent private F value. Every selected FORS secret also remains an independent F value. Independence is a prior joint statement before observing the generated root/cache, not a claim about the posterior conditioned on them.

**PROVED — ordinary PRE program B_PRE.** Ask one target at T_p in stage one, then receive S and run all source phases. For selected WOTS p=0 place opaque X at the selected F value. For p>0 sample selected F and replacement x_1,...,x_(p-1) normally, put opaque X at x_p, and set the actual suffix start x_(p+1)=Y, the challenge image. All later positions are computed with actual h. Use the same complete-response guard e<=p. For FORS use the earlier opaque-secret/leaf-image Y adapter. Logical caches, roots, counters and all unguarded responses are fully computed from the selected endpoint/image, with unchanged source F/J/S law. No source internal call is required to consume the unknown bytes X.

**PROVED — PRE extraction.** Run the actual final classical source verifier. At any reached selected T_p call with payload z satisfying Th(S,T_p,z)=Y, output (target index 1,z). B may require source Win before extracting by retaining candidate bytes and checking acceptance/root equality. It need not decide whether z=X; any preimage is valid. On an E_254 run the selected call is exactly z=X, hence a preimage exists in its final classical trace, and the guard did not abort. Thus Pr[G_254:E_254]<=Succ_SM-PRE(Th_128^h,1;B_PRE). No h query from quantum A is extracted or measured.

**PROVED — whole final-hidden event inequality.** Let Delta_k=Adv_SM-UD(Th_128^h,1;B_UD,k). Telescoping the scalar probabilities, using Pr[G_k:E_k]<=Pr[G_(k+1):E_(k+1)]+Delta_k, gives

Pr_HJ[h][Win AND H_final]
  <= 2^55 ( Succ_SM-PRE(Th_128^h,1;B_PRE) + sum_(k=0)^253 Delta_k ).

There are 254 possible hops because the largest eligible position is 254 at width 256; width-16 chains stop at p=14 and FORS at p=0. The formula uses actual advantages of explicit ordinary primitive adversaries, not an independent random oracle or a numeric RO bound. Earlier parent hybrids restricting S!=0 can carry that conjunction throughout and fail locally at zero; the local construction itself is well-defined for every S under the supplied F/J independence premise.

## 5. Resources and limitations

**COMPUTED / PROVED — localized overhead.** Each primitive adversary makes one target request, zero stage-one collection requests, and simulates at most one source execution. Selection/unranking uses bounded integers, not a D_H table. A selected chain has at most 256 Node16 positions. At most 4096 bytes of selected-chain node payload plus constant marker/address metadata suffice. Precomputing its known endpoint from challenge Y needs at most 254 additional public hashes; overwritten internal prefix evaluations need not be performed. Its optional selected private F preparation adds at most one private dictionary label; hence F distinct cap min(D_0,1+2,181,038,080+q*U_mode), with U_hot=12,816, U_cold=33,567,232 and D_0=19,212,937,084,272,640. J<=q. These are extra selected-chain resources; real source tree/cache and dictionary/message allocations remain charged.

**COMPUTED — retained conservative complete-h cap.** Each B is bounded by Q_h+H_setup+q*H_mode+3,224+254 complete h evaluations, with H_setup=9,007,240,159,690,751, H_hot=21,474,918,570 and H_cold=34,330,460,329. These caps cover complete-message hashes, outer/canonical u32 counter rows and actual source setup/signing/final verification work. The hybrid's internal replacement can remove evaluations but cannot increase these upper caps, apart from the selected helper. Noncompletion follows absorbing totalization rather than success conditioning. Quantum A's actual public-h circuit/register/time resources remain part of B. Challenge hashing/storage is separately attributable to the challenger. No feasible execution or concrete advantage follows from these finite caps.
