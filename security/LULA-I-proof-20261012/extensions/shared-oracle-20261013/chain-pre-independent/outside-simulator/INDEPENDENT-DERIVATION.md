## 2. Finite injective byte-string encoding

**PROVED.** For byte words w of length ell<=b, define
enc(w)=sum_{j=0}^{ell-1}256^j+BE_integer(w).
The length-ell block is disjoint from every other length block, so empty/leading-zero words are distinct. Range is0..V_b-1, V_b=sum_{ell=0}^b256^ell. For b>=1, ceil(log2 V_b)=8b+1; V_0=1. Let m be the least power of2 at least max(256,8b+1) for b>=1 (at least256 for b0). Then V_b<=2^m and m<2*max(256,8b+1) unless that maximum is already a power of2. ## 3. Deterministic field and modulus construction

**PROVED (quadratic tower).** Construct GF2, then fields of degrees s=1,2,4,...,m. At a stage with field K of2^s elements, its absolute trace Tr(a)=sum_{j=0}^{s-1}a^(2^j) is a nonzero GF2-linear map into GF2. It is nonzero because this nonzero polynomial has degree2^(s-1)<2^s and cannot vanish at every field element. Frobenius shows Tr(a)^2=Tr(a), hence values0/1. Therefore at least one of the s standard coordinate basis elements has trace1; deterministically scan them.

For such a, Z^2+Z+a is irreducible over K: the image of z->z^2+z has kernel{0,1}, size2^(s-1), and equals the trace-zero subspace. Trace(a)=1 excludes a root. The quotient is a field of2^(2s) elements with explicit two-component representation. In that extension, multiplication uses A=uu', B=vv', C=(u+v)(u'+v') and D=aB, giving low A+D and high C+A. Four recursive base-field multiplications plus O(s) additions yield O(s^2) bit cost by induction. Trace computation costs O(s^3); scanning its s basis candidates costs O(s^4). Summing doubling stages is O(m^4) deterministic time, with conservative O(m^2) bit workspace and O(m) stored tower constants. No randomized retry, expected-only runtime, or exponential candidate enumeration is assumed.

**PROVED (single binary modulus if needed).** Let z be the generator added at the last tower stage. It is not in the embedded GF2^(m/2). Its Frobenius orbit has full length m: any shorter orbit length divides the power-of-two m and hence divides m/2, which would make z a root of X^(2^(m/2))-X and thus a member of that embedded subfield, contradiction. Therefore P_m(X)=product_{j=0}^{m-1}(X-z^(2^j)) is a monic irreducible binary polynomial of degree m. Its coefficients are Frobenius-fixed, hence0/1. Compute the conjugates and multiply linear factors in the already constructed tower field; O(m^2) field operations with O(m^2) bit multiplication cost gives O(m^4) deterministic bit work and O(m^2) workspace. Use this P_m for ordinary polynomial-basis field arithmetic afterward. This supplies an actual deterministic field-modulus construction, not a claim that a suitable irreducible polynomial is available for free.

## 4. k-wise polynomial family and full256 projection

**PROVED.** Set k=min(2T,V_b). Choose k independent uniform m-bit coefficients a0,...,a_(k-1) in GF2^m. Let F_a(w)=proj256(sum_{j=0}^{k-1}a_j enc(w)^j), where proj256 is any fixed256-coordinate surjective linear projection, serialized as32 bytes with source prefix/lower limbs retained. At t<=k distinct encoded points, the first t coefficients give an invertible Vandermonde map; its determinant is the product of nonzero pairwise differences. Conditional on remaining coefficients, the t full field outputs are independent uniform. Projection preserves independent uniform256-bit outputs. If k=V_b, every domain output is jointly independent, giving exactly the entire ordinary RF law; otherwise the2T-wise replacement fact gives exactly the compiled T-query behavior. Coefficients use k*m independent random bits and k*m stored bits, not256V_b bits.

**PROVED (reversible evaluation).** Horner evaluation uses O(k) field multiplications/additions. Naive carryless multiplication followed by reduction modulo P_m costs O(m^2) bit gates. Reversibly compute successive Horner values into fresh m-bit registers, XOR the final256-bit projection into the response, and reverse the computation; save O(km) intermediate bits, plus polynomial-space multiplication/index work. This realizes a clean XOR oracle using O(km^2+b^2) elementary reversible gates and O(km+m^2+b^2) work/key bits. It does not assume in-place multiplication by possiblyzero input is reversible. Initialization charges O(m^4+km+b^2) deterministic bit operations and km random bits, including field construction and coefficient writes. All coherent, honest/classical, preparation and final calls use this SAME fixed family member.
