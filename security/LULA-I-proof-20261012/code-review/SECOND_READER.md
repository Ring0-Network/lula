**Precision notes / suggested clarification, not substantive rejection.**

1. `DERIVATION.md` says targeting a bucket “loses its 1/B selection factor.” Clarify this as **must still pay the probability 1/B of selecting that bucket per uniform full-digest query**. Given a transcript, targeting bucket b yields `(1/(B t)) product_j |C_(b,j)|/t`; targeting any covered bucket yields the stated sum over b. This avoids reading “loses” as permission to drop the denominator.
2. The 2^-128 inclusive WOTS quantity assumes a fresh independent raw digest trial at the addressed message hash, before conditioning on acceptance. Conditional on the sum slice it is `1/|slice|`, with slice sizes and acceptance exponents reported separately. Neither value is already the code's complete forgery probability.
