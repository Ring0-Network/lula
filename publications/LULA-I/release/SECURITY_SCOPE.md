# Scientific and operational scope

The full reference-signer security bound remains open. The conditional rollback transfer result preserves an applicable reference bound under the stated interface; it does not supply that bound. The 2^40-query objective and component-model exponents are not certified capacity or full 128-bit security claims.

Honest prepared, restored and absent caches preserve one partial signing function for successfully generated keys. Canonical searches may fail to terminate within their finite counter space. The theorem's observations occur between completed calls. Corrupted-cache writes, partial-call snapshots, compromise of the key or implementation, side channels and general operational deployment guarantees require additional analysis.

Public test vectors and deterministic fixture-generation recipes are verification material. They do not contain private keys suitable for operational use. A saved public signature is evidence of a valid encoding, not proof of unforgeability. No new signing attack is launched by the reproduction commands.

Funding, competing interests and the correspondence of historical timed binaries to source/build inputs remain factual questions. The bundle does not invent declarations or recover missing historical binary provenance. No security response policy, maintenance service or deployment recommendation is implied by this research artifact.
