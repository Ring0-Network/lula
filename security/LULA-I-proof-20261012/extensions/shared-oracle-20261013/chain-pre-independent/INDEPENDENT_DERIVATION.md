## Source fact and obstruction

**PROVED (source binding).** WotsC::chain uses a distinct type0 address for every chain position. In HJ the ghost sampler independently chooses every position X_p, fixes O_d(X_p)=(X_{p+1},rho_p), and keeps residual complete256 replies independent. Hidden means not yet disclosed by a completed signature; disclosure includes its entire forward suffix. FORS leaves are analogous independent inputs. Honest child values, accepted type7 counters and route decisions read only public surface, outside planes and independent J, never hidden chain coordinates. S nonzero prevents unaddressed long H_msg inputs from aliasing protected typed planes. This is the same model as the reviewed hidden-input theorem, including collisions between numerically equal coordinates.

**PROVED (perfect-simulation obstruction).** An interior PRE challenge hides X_p and supplies Y_p=O_p(X_p). A reduction knowing X_{p-1} cannot provide the honest full reply at O_{p-1}(X_{p-1}), whose prefix must be the unknown X_p. Knowing the target image or marginal uniformity of X_p does not solve that problem. A nonaborting whole-game simulator that samples an independent prefix Z there agrees only when Z=X_p, probability 1/N, or requires an additional programming/inversion interface. This refutes that shortcut, not all PRE reductions.
