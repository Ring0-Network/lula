## Regeneration and preserved mechanism

`python -I -B scripts/integrate_comparison.py` reads the frozen cut and audited rows, regenerates comparison-results.tex, two vector figures, integrated-row JSON and process-memory JSON, and launches no signer. Tables round milliseconds to three decimal places. Medians average the central pair; p95/p99 use nearest rank. Raw integral nanoseconds and actual process-memory bytes remain available.

The private package preserves controller/adapters, raw observations, vectors, manifests, source references and audit scripts. It does not reconstruct every original upstream binary/library build input: original locators and declared commit/digest records must be recovered before a matched timing rerun. Digest records alone are not compiler-input equivalence proofs.

The exact construction, three proofs, five algorithms, two original diagrams and threat-model premises are preserved. Public identity and filenames remain LULA I; no internal label enters the manuscript or public figures. No ePrint submission is performed.
