# Hidden chain and FORS values: independent ghost derivation

**Scope.** Saved before reading a peer's hidden-value or WOTS-certification report. This is a classical statistical coupling attempt for an explicitly restricted ghost model. It preserves the source and all earlier packets. A complete source reduction, efficient simulator and full EUF-CMA conclusion are not asserted.

## 1. Binding, base hybrid and protected coordinates

Address/hash encoding is at 60–95 and 121–187; chain construction/signing at 239–276; FORS construction/revelation at 347–394; completed signing at 601–668. **ASSUMED (base game).** Begin after joint privatization of the type-5/type-6 seed derivations and message randomizer, with independent memoized full-image maps F(A), J(m). Their first 16 bytes are used by the source. Their raw secret-key encodings are not silently added back to the adversary's public oracle. That earlier real-to-ideal loss is separate. This new argument does not infer that actual publicly queried secret-suffix derivations remain independent after a master-key guess.

**ASSUMED (interface).** Fixed single-root LULA I parameters, one shared complete-hash RO, nonzero public seed, atomic completed-call signatures, honest snapshots, no private-context/fixture/cache/timing/intermediate-query disclosure. The source's internal chain/tree hashing is not a public transcript of its inputs. Retain the partial-source convention: no further public interaction after a nonterminating signing request. Legitimate knowledge accumulates across all branches and is never erased by Restore.

**PROVED (coordinate inventory).** For every WOTS key a=(layer,tree,kp), chain i and position p=0…w−1, let X_(a,i,p) be the honest chain value. Position zero is the private type-5 map prefix; position p+1 is the prefix of the type-0 full image at payload X_(a,i,p). The type-0 address includes i,p, so each step is a different restriction. For each real FORS leaf f=(tree,kp,global_leaf), let S_f be its private type-6 prefix; its type-3 height-zero image is L_f. These private-map labels are distinct. Every source repetition at a coordinate returns the same value.

## 2. Eager hidden graph with limited public surface

**PROVED (sampler).** Sample all WOTS X_(a,i,p) and FORS S_f as independent uniform 128-bit values. For each type-0 coordinate p<w−1, sample a full image `(X_(a,i,p+1),rho_(a,i,p))`, with an independent uniform lower 128-bit limb rho. At its designated payload X_(a,i,p), the real oracle returns that exact full image. For each FORS leaf sample independent full image `(L_f,lambda_f)` and assign it at S_f in its height-zero type-3 domain. Generate endpoint compressions and Merkle/FORS internal/root images in topological order, using independent full images at their designated inputs. Keep every residual input image independently uniform. This is the correct typed-RO marginal: each designated payload depends only on predecessors, not its own restriction. The private map F prefixes are the corresponding position-zero or FORS secret variables; their unused lower limbs are sampled consistently once.

**PROVED (position separation).** The source includes chain position p in address word 3. Different positions therefore have different restrictions even if two sampled values coincide numerically. No assumption that all graph values are distinct is made. Replacing this addressed chain by repeated iteration of one unsalted function would require additional cycle/repeated-input analysis and would not preserve this exact independent-value sampler.

**PROVED / essential restriction.** Do not reveal the previous task's entire designated-payload graph. It reveals all private chain starts and FORS secrets. Instead give a stronger *public surface* consisting of all WOTS endpoints X_(a,i,w−1), all compressed WOTS PK images, all Merkle node images, all FORS leaf images L_f and internal/root-compression images. This may exceed the actual public key and signatures, but contains neither unrevealed FORS secret inputs nor interior/predecessor chain values. These public outputs and endpoints are independent of the still-hidden predecessor coordinates in the eager model. An event bound holding with this extra public information also bounds the original adversary which ignores it.

**PROVED (initial known set).** Mark all endpoints known. Mark no interior WOTS value or FORS secret known initially. Public compression/parent designated inputs contain only the strengthened public surface, so their oracle answers can be installed consistently from the start. Type-0 predecessor points and type-3 secret-leaf points remain hidden until legitimate disclosure.

## 3. Continued ideal oracle and legitimate exposure

**PROVED (ideal rule).** The public ideal oracle answers an unprogrammed raw point from its ordinary memoized uniform full-image table. It checks only publicly known/programmed points when selecting an answer. An analytical hidden-hit flag may test whether a raw point's header identifies a currently hidden coordinate and its payload equals that coordinate's value. The flag must never alter a response, program a point, reveal a value, choose a branch or stop the continued ideal execution.

**PROVED (FORS exposure).** A completed signature reveals exactly its selected S_f for each of the 17 real trees. Mark these coordinate/value pairs known permanently and install their full designated leaf images at those newly known points if the public table has not already answered them. Repeated exposure of the same leaf adds no new information or point. Revealing the leaf image L_f earlier was harmless to this ideal noninterference because it was sampled independently of S_f.

**PROVED (WOTS exposure).** A completed signature at key a has its canonical digest vector e. Reveal X_(a,i,e_i) and, for a stronger disclosure model, all its forward suffix values through the endpoint. Mark positions p≥e_i known; install the stored full type-0 images at those known suffix inputs. This emulates what public forward hashing from the signature can obtain. A previously revealed endpoint is not a hidden hit. For honest repeated use of a, the same child value and first accepted counter give the same vector, so the same suffix is revealed again. There is no second distinct honest vector.

**PROVED (no overwrite).** If a newly known designated point already has a public reply, keep that earlier reply in the continued ideal table. Never overwrite it. On the coupled good history, such a point could not have been queried while hidden: that would already have been a hidden hit. Thus legitimate installation is consistent whenever agreement is required. After a previously charged hit, source and ideal may disagree and ideal signatures may fail ordinary verification; this is permitted in a game-distance coupling. The conflict decision and retained reply use only now-revealed values and previous public data, not any still-hidden coordinate or the analytical flag.

**PROVED (full-image consistency).** Every install uses the complete stored 32-byte image, not a fresh lower half. A known chain suffix query returns the same prefix and lower limb on every repetition. For a newly exposed last predecessor, its returned prefix is the already known endpoint. Future disclosure cannot repair an earlier uncharged mismatch by reprogramming.

## 4. Predictable disclosure and independence

**ASSUMED (abstract disclosure condition).** A decision about which coordinates to reveal must depend on public surface/coins, private J values independent of the protected coordinates, previous public ideal answers and already revealed values. It cannot inspect a still-hidden coordinate to choose the reveal index. The set of known coordinates is monotone. For WOTS, the rule reveals a suffix; for FORS, selected individual inputs. This condition is substantive, not implied by the word “adaptive.”

**PROVED (continued-ideal invariant under that condition).** Conditional on an unflagged public ideal history, all remaining hidden coordinate values are independent uniform 128-bit variables. Initially this follows from the sampler. A public raw reply reads no hidden variable. A predictable index choice does not bias the selected coordinate; publishing its value leaves the others independent. Installing known-point replies uses only revealed values and independent stored image limbs; retained-table conflicts likewise use only revealed values and old public data. Induction gives the invariant even after analytical hits, provided the flag does not change ideal execution. Do not condition on “no earlier hit” and then assume unchanged full uniformity; that conditioning removes tested values.

**PROVED (source disclosure selector in the ghost model).** The source's honest WOTS child is the fixed FORS-root-compression image at layer zero or the fixed child-subtree root at upper layers. These are strengthened public-surface images, not interior values of that key's chains. Its type-7 counter plane is disjoint from type-0/type-3 hidden-input domains. The first accepted counter and digit vector therefore depend on that public child image and the type-7 public table, not on still-hidden predecessor values. H_msg routing uses independent J and public hash replies with the fixed public root. Nonzero public seed excludes an unaddressed inner input from the typed hidden headers; outer inputs are only 52 bytes. Thus route/leaf selection is also a predictable disclosure decision in this specified ideal game. Repeated source addresses and honest restoration do not change this rule.

**PROVED (atomic completion rule).** Reveal the selected variables only after all searches for that signing request complete. Whether the metadata searches complete is determined by those outside/public planes and values, not protected predecessor coordinates. A nonterminating call emits no FORS secret or intermediate WOTS value despite computing them privately. Treating the source's private output buffer as public before completion would invalidate this disclosure rule and is outside the stated interface.

**OPEN (exact refinement / broader interfaces).** This is a manually derived source-model selector argument, not a checked Rust refinement. An exposed private hash-call transcript, secret-bearing fixture, arbitrary corrupted counter policy or source state selecting indices from hidden values would require a new invariant. The primitive privatization and public-surface augmentation must be connected explicitly when composing this argument with the original game.

## 5. Address-specific hidden hits and scalar budgets

**PROVED (event).** A hidden hit is an evaluation of a type-0 raw point `(a,i,p,payload)` with p currently hidden and payload equal to X_(a,i,p), or a height-zero type-3 FORS point whose secret is currently hidden and payload equals S_f. This is coordinate-specific genuine-input acquisition before its authorized disclosure. It is not “some byte string in the adversary's memory equals any secret anywhere,” which could incur a large target-set factor and is not the required typed verifier event.

**PROVED (meaning of hidden).** “Hidden” means that this indexed coordinate has not been legitimately disclosed, not that its numeric value cannot coincidentally equal another revealed value. Such coincidences are allowed. A copied revealed value submitted at another still-hidden coordinate is a candidate there and is counted if correct; the header supplies the coordinate association necessary for the scalar bound.

**PROVED (per-query bound).** A raw header identifies at most one protected coordinate. Conditional on the unflagged ideal history, a candidate payload selected for a currently hidden coordinate matches with probability 2^-128. A known suffix/leaf query has probability cost zero in this hidden-hit ledger and is answered consistently. A repeated candidate need not be counted as a new opportunity; a conservative bound counting all raw queries remains valid. No total-coordinate multiplier is needed for this precisely addressed event.

**PROVED (adaptive union).** Over B addressed candidate evaluations before authorized disclosure, `Pr[hidden hit] <= min(1,B/2^128)`. This follows by summing conditional hit indicators in the continued unflagged ideal execution. The sampled variables can cease being hidden only through predictable disclosure; the hit flag is not used to choose the schedule. If a raw prequery reaches a value that will be legitimately revealed later, it is already a hit at the earlier time. Future revelation does not erase it.

**PROVED (final verification budget).** A final LULA I verification offers at most 3,017 potential protected-point candidates: 17 FORS secret-leaf checks, four lower layers with 240 chain hashes each, and 2,040 top chain hashes. Authentication/PK compression and internal FORS nodes are not protected-value inputs here. The five digest checks and message hashing are outside this hidden ledger. Earlier source-independent work accounting gives 3,217 selected public-graph calls total; do not confuse that collision budget with the narrower 3,017 protected-input budget.

**PROVED (conditional statistical result).** In the specified nonzero-seed base hybrid, with Q_public raw adversarial calls and one final verification,

    Pr[coordinate-specific hidden hit] <= min(1,(Q_public+3017)/2^128).

Until the first such hit, the source-programmed raw oracle and the continued ideal oracle agree: already known points use their stored images, hidden designated points have not been publicly evaluated, and every other point uses the same residual table. Thus this is also a conservative event-distance loss for the defined hidden-point idealization. Uniform zero-public-seed can be charged separately by adding 2^-128 in an unconditioned game; avoid charging it again if the earlier shared-oracle hybrid already did so.

**PROVED (zero-seed / augmentation qualification).** If charging the zero-seed branch here, mark agreement lost immediately and retain the actual base game's shared public-oracle marginal there; do not pretend the nonzero-header exclusions hold. The stronger public surface is optional auxiliary information given consistently in both sides of this component coupling. A source adversary can be embedded by ignoring it; projecting the augmented transcripts recovers its original event probabilities. This observation does not claim that the earlier primitive privatization was originally stated for every possible auxiliary exposure, or that full designated payloads are safe to reveal.

## 6. Genuine evidence versus other forgery branches

**PROVED (classification boundary).** For a forged WOTS vector y, matching the honest endpoint while beginning with a value different from the genuine X_y creates an alternative-input image hit somewhere along that addressed chain, unless a separate endpoint-compression substitution already occurred. Those are public-graph target events from a different lemma. Beginning with genuine X_y when y is below the revealed suffix threshold is a hidden hit. For an unqueried key the only initially known positions are endpoints: an accepted target-sum vector cannot put every digit at w−1, so at least one genuine predecessor would be hidden. No canonical counter needs to exist at that unqueried key for this statement.

**PROVED (queried key boundary).** For a honestly exposed canonical vector e, forward-reachable positions y≥e and equal sums force y=e. This does not rule out a new child/counter hashing to the same vector; that is the separate type-7 certification term. Similarly, a FORS original secret already revealed at its coordinate is a coverage case, an unrevealed original secret is a hidden hit, and a different secret with the same leaf image is a public target-image event. The hidden lemma does not classify the complete forgery game by itself.

## 7. Partial searches and optional global existence event

**PROVED (partial version needs no global existence assumption).** Define canonical vectors only for completed legitimate exposures. If a counter plane has no accepted 32-bit counter, the corresponding source call does not produce an exposure or further public response in the declared partial interface. An unqueried key keeps every predecessor hidden and its endpoint known; no vector is assigned to it merely to prove the genuine-predecessor condition. Setup itself emits no secret-value disclosures. No totality or success-conditioned normalization is inferred.

**PROVED (optional all-key event, ideal type-7 model).** If another proof wants a canonical vector at every address before interaction, let N_L=2^41+2^36+2^31+2^26=2,269,957,324,800 lower WOTS keys and N_T=2^21=2,097,152 top keys. With exact sum-slice densities p_L and p_T and independent typed RO planes at fixed child values, an unconditioned union bound on absent accepted counters is

    delta_all <= N_L(1−p_L)^(2^32) + N_T(1−p_T)^(2^32).

The public child value is independent of its own type-7 restriction and all 2^32 counter payloads are distinct. This is a model event, not a finite failure response implemented by the source. Full-key counts are necessary if one actually assumes global existence; a q-only bound cannot justify a preexisting total map over all keys. A setup-only requirement involves top configured addresses, not all lower keys. This argument supplies no corresponding unaddressed H_msg exhaustion theorem.

**COMPUTED / PROVED (conservative exact model bound).** Integer coefficient DP gives lower slice count `5181241160064611531897369560287267312` and top slice count `454918678781617793203528879683071744`, both at least 2^128/1024. Also N_L+N_T<2^42. Since `(1−1/1024)^1024<1/2` by the binomial/Bernoulli inequality for its reciprocal, each 2^32-counter exhaustion probability is at most 2^(-2^22). Therefore the optional all-key union event is bounded by `2^(-4194262)`; a top-only setup union is bounded by `2^(-4194283)`. These are deliberately conservative exact consequences of the typed-RO model, not observed source failure probabilities or a proof of totality for fixed SHA-256.

**OPEN (conditioning/efficiency/QROM).** Normalizing on successful setup needs an explicit conditional-game argument. Lazy ghost sampling avoids allocating the enormous variable family, but does not yield an efficient complete signer or a polynomial worst-case simulator for partial searches. A quantum query cannot be treated as one literal candidate. The mathematical invariant, source-model bridge and any accepted-counter/global event require independent review before inclusion in a final composed bound.

**Final status.** The proposed hidden-value term is justified under the explicitly predictable disclosure/continued-ideal hypothesis, with source semantics supplying a plausible manual bridge in the already privatized nonzero-seed game. It does not reuse secret-leaking full-payload exposure, does not charge known suffixes, and does not promote a generic SPR assertion to full security. A finite control must test predictable disclosure and demonstrate failure when the selector reads an unrevealed value.
