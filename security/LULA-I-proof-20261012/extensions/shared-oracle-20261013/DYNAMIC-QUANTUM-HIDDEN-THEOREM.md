## 2. Two oracle channels on the same sampled environment

**PROVED — REAL.** O_G is the ordinary fixed fully programmed oracle from the sampler. Honest services evaluate their private graph G and metadata with its ordinary semantics. The set Known records permanently exposed FORS secrets and WOTS suffix coordinates across every branch. **PROVED — IDEAL overlay definition.** O_Known agrees with O_G at all nonprotected designated graph points and every protected coordinate in Known. All replies within a fixed Known epoch come from this complete function. Updating Known changes the oracle by overlaying newly revealed points with their genuine full images. O_0 itself is never resampled or overwritten.

This IDEAL oracle is deliberately time-dependent and may disagree with an earlier reply after disclosure. It is not claimed to be an ordinary static RO. Detectable disturbance from pre-disclosure coherent queries is charged by the state-distance argument; no claim of exact classical no-overwrite consistency is used. ## 3. Continued IDEAL unused-coordinate invariant

**PROVED — proposed invariant.** Conditional on the public safe surface, independent outside/random tables, measured public history and already disclosed coordinate values, remaining protected coordinates are independent uniform and unused by the adversary's quantum density operator. This does not condition on avoiding a hit. The analytical genuine-hit predicate cannot alter IDEAL responses or control flow.

Initially the sampler gives this factorization. A coherent O_Known query depends only on O_0, nonprotected public graph data and known coordinate/value pairs. It therefore does not read a remaining hidden variable. Identical local operations/measurements preserve that independence. Honest metadata chooses coordinates to disclose without reading still-hidden inputs: fixed child values and type7/public message replies belong to the permitted planes. Revealing a predictable subset leaves undisclosed coordinates uniform. Suffix closure ensures a newly programmed chain point's successor is also known, so its entire genuine full image uses no remaining hidden prefix. Independent image suffixes and exposed values can be installed without reading another hidden coordinate. An overlay depending on an undisclosed successor would break this invariant.

Do not condition on the complete private F table or full latent graph while making this independence statement: those tables contain protected coordinates. The inaccessible environment stores them for the mathematical coupling; the per-query expectation averages over their unused values. Arbitrary coherent signing requests or hidden-driven digit selection are excluded.

## 4. Coherent-query channel discrepancy

**PROVED — elementary support estimate.** On a shared IDEAL prefix, the two public XOR oracles differ only at currently hidden designated points. A literal raw register word identifies at most one protected coordinate, and its guessed16byte payload matches that unused coordinate with probability1/N. For an arbitrary entangled query state psi, let mu be its input mass on those points. Outside that support the oracle unitaries agree; on it their operator-norm difference is at most2. Hence ||(U_G−U_Known)psi||<=2sqrt(mu). The averaged IDEAL mass obeys E[mu]<=1/N, so convexity/Cauchy–Schwarz give at most2/sqrt(N) trace-distance discrepancy per active coherent query.

**PROVED — adaptive schedule candidate.** Include sampled coins/tables and Known in the inaccessible classical environment. CPTP contraction and product telescoping evaluate each difference on the continued IDEAL prefix, never the REAL survivor posterior. Decompose measured scheduling histories; activation probabilities multiply their conditional cost rather than entering a square root. The pathwise Q cap then gives D(prefix_REAL,prefix_IDEAL)<=min(1,2Q/sqrt(N)) before final verification. Honest disclosure channels are identical as argued above; q does not multiply this quantum-query term. Noncompletion branches act identically and cannot win.

**PROVED — purified query-slot refinement candidate.** Preserve copies of sampled classical coins and every measurement/history register in an inaccessible purifying environment, using the SAME service/local isometries in both processes. For a prefix immediately before a coherent query, the squared support norm equals its averaged hidden-input mass, at most1/N. Hence that query's entire purified vector discrepancy is at most2/sqrt(N), without Jensen loss beyond this expectation. Group all identical adaptive classical service/local operations between the k-th and (k+1)-st adversarial coherent query into one common isometry. Pad halted/no-further-query branches with a raw empty-string query (outside protected domains) or an XOR query acting on |+> response qubits. There are Q such slots, regardless of how many service calls occur between them. Do NOT sum sqrt(activation probabilities) across Q+q individual scheduling steps; that would overcharge sparse query positions. Product telescoping through the packed common isometries gives ||Psi_REAL−Psi_IDEAL||<=2Q/sqrt(N). This stronger vector statement, not merely trace distance, supports the square-root success bound below.
